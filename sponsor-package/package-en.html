01Who we are

A student association built around technical excellence.

Founded by computer science students at UQAM, AGEEI exists to champion the professional and intellectual growth of our community through events, competitions, and the partnerships that make them possible.

We represent every student in UQAM's Bachelor's and Certificates in Computer Science and Software Engineering. From first-year students discovering algorithms to graduating engineers shipping production code, our members share one thing: a deep commitment to building well.

03Why sponsor AGEEI

Five reasons to sponsor AGEEI.

01

Be there before they're on the market.

Our faculty team placed 2nd at NorthSec in 2026 after two back-to-back wins, and our members top CSGames leaderboards. Most companies only meet them at graduation. Sponsorship puts you in the room while they're still students.

02

Recruiting built on familiarity.

The students who remember your brand from a workshop or a judging panel are the ones who apply, accept, and stay. Our events are where those impressions form, before anyone has sent a cold recruiter email.

03

A relationship, not a logo placement.

Students remember who showed up for them: not logos, but people. Send your engineers to judge, mentor, and teach. That's the affinity that lasts a career.

04

Real feedback from people who build.

Run an atelier on your stack or seed your APIs into student projects. You get genuine usage data and candid feedback from builders who engage with your tech on its own merits, not through a sales filter.

05

Start small. The relationship is the return.

A single atelier puts your engineers in front of 30 students for an evening. No six-figure commitment required. That's often how our best partnerships start.

04Our four flagship events

Four ways to show up for our community.

Each event reaches a different audience and serves a different sponsorship goal. Mix and match.

Event 01: Recruiting { : }

Career Fair

Our largest single-day recruiting event. Booths, scheduled interview slots, a CV booklet distributed to every sponsor, and a captive audience of CS students looking for internships and full-time roles.

1day Wintersemester UQAMon-campus
Event 02: Engagement { ; }

Hackathon

A weekend-long build event where students prototype, demo, and compete. Sponsor a technical, product, or design challenge, send mentors and judges, run a workshop, and watch your problem space turn into working ideas in real time.

3 daysbuild time Wintersemester Opensponsor challenges
Event 03: Brand & Education { ! }

Company Atelier

An evening or half-day deep dive on a topic your team owns: AI, cloud, security, dev tooling. Hands-on, technical, and led by your specialists. Strong fit for partners with a clear technical story to tell.
